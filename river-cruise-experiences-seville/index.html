FAQ

River cruises in Sevilla: common questions

How many guests can a boat event host?+

From intimate groups of up to 12 on the luxury yacht, to 50 on the solar boat and more than 100 guests on the river cruise boat. For larger groups, several boats can sail together.

Can we have dinner on board?+

Yes. We arrange catering of local Spanish dishes, from a light cocktail with tapas to a seated dinner, depending on the boat and your group size.

Which monuments do we see from the river?+

A cruise on the Guadalquivir passes some of Seville’s most important sights, including the Torre del Oro, the Plaza de Toros, the Triana Bridge and Calle Betis.

What is the best time for a river cruise?+

Sunset is the most popular time, with golden light over the city and the monuments lighting up as you return. In summer, evening cruises also avoid the heat of the day.

Can we celebrate a wedding on a boat?+

Yes. We organise wedding ceremonies, receptions and dinners on board, including flowers, décor, catering and live music. We can also arrange a boat for a welcome drink or an after-party for your wedding guests.

Can a boat event be combined with other activities?+

Yes. A cruise works well as a welcome drink before dinner, as part of a gala evening or after a kayak or city activity during the day.
