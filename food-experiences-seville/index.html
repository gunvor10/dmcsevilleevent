FAQ

Food experiences in Seville: common questions

Can you cater for dietary requirements?+

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal and allergy requirements are planned in advance with every restaurant, chef and producer.

How far from Seville are the farm and bodega visits?+

Olive oil farms and Iberian pig farms are within about an hour of Seville. Sherry bodegas are in Jerez, around one hour by coach, and there are also wineries closer to the city.

Can a tapas tour work for a large group?+

Yes. Large groups are split into smaller teams with their own guide and route, so each group can enjoy the real tapas bars without crowding them.

Can food experiences be used for team building?+

Yes. Cooking classes, paella competitions and tapas challenges are some of our most popular team building activities.

Can we combine several experiences in one programme?+

Yes. Our Gastro Weekend Break and Tailor-Made Food Tour combine markets, cooking, tastings and dinners into one programme built around your group.
