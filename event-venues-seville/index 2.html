THE RIGHT SETTING

In Seville, the venue is part of the experience

Few cities offer such a variety of settings within a few kilometres: Renaissance palaces with Mudéjar patios, whitewashed haciendas among olive groves, terraces facing the Giralda and boats on the Guadalquivir.

Many of the most beautiful spaces are private and not listed anywhere. As your DMC in Seville, we know the owners, the capacities and the practical details, and we handle permits, catering, décor, sound and transport so your venue works as well as it looks.

PLANNER’S CHECKLIST

What to consider when choosing a venue in Seville

01
Season and heat

Outdoor patios and rooftops are at their best from spring to autumn. In July and August we plan open-air events for the evening.

02
Access and transport

Coaches cannot enter much of the old town. We plan drop-off points, short walks or transfers so guests arrive relaxed.

03
Permits and noise

Monuments and historic buildings have their own rules on music, catering and closing times. We know them in advance.

04
Plan B

Rain is rare in Seville, but every outdoor event has a covered or indoor alternative at the same venue.

FAQ

Event venues in Seville: common questions

Can we book a palace or monument exclusively for our group?+

Yes. Many private palaces can be booked exclusively, and some monuments can be used outside opening hours, depending on availability, permits and the type of event.

What is the largest group you can host in Seville?+

Haciendas, hotels and conference centres can host events for more than 1,000 guests. For very large groups we often combine a reception in one venue with dinner in another.

Do you charge for venue finding?+

When DMC Seville Event organises your event, our venue search is included in the service.

How far in advance should we book a venue?+

For spring and autumn, and for the most exclusive palaces, we recommend booking six to twelve months ahead. Other dates can often be arranged at shorter notice.

Can we visit the venues before booking?+

Yes. We organise site inspections in Seville, and we can also send photos, videos and floor plans in advance.
