Andalusia’s Capital

Seville is Andalusia’s capital and largest city, where Moorish, Gothic and Renaissance architecture sit side by side across a walkable historic centre, one of the largest in Europe.

The Real Alcázar & Cathedral

The Real Alcázar, a UNESCO-listed royal palace still in use today, and the Cathedral with its Giralda bell tower are among Spain’s most visited monuments, offering dramatic settings for private group visits.

Flamenco & Tapas Culture

Flamenco was born in this region, and Seville’s taverns and tapas bars make for an authentic evening program, from flamenco tablaos to tapas crawls through Triana and Santa Cruz.

A City Built for Events

With palaces, exhibition halls and hotels built for the 1929 and 1992 international expos, Seville has more large-scale MICE infrastructure than anywhere else in Andalusia.

Triana & the Guadalquivir

Across the river, the Triana quarter offers a more local side of the city — ceramic workshops, riverside bars and views back over the skyline, popular for smaller group evenings.

Excellent Connections

Seville Airport (SVQ) has direct flights to major European cities, making it the easiest Andalusian gateway for international groups arriving from multiple countries.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Sevilla

What is the nearest airport to Seville for a corporate event?
Seville Airport (SVQ) is the nearest airport, roughly 10 km from the city centre, with direct connections to major European hubs.
How many people can DMC Seville Event handle for a MICE program in Seville?
We regularly organise corporate meetings and incentive groups from 20 to over 300 participants, adapting venues and logistics to group size.
What is included in a 4-day incentive program in Seville?
A typical program includes airport transfers, a corporate meeting day, guided cultural visits, a team building activity and a gala dinner, all fully customisable to your goals and budget.
What is the best time of year for an incentive trip to Seville?
Spring and autumn offer the most comfortable temperatures, though Seville’s festivals and long evenings make it a strong destination year-round.
What team building activities are available in Seville?
Popular options include treasure hunts through Santa Cruz, flamenco workshops and cooking classes centred on Andalusian cuisine.
Can Seville be combined with other Andalusian destinations?
Yes — Seville is the natural base for a multi-destination program, easily combined with Córdoba, Cádiz or Huelva.
