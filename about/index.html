About DMC Seville Event

FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Gunvor Guttormsen

I'm Gunvor Guttormsen, a Norwegian-born traveler, storyteller and licensed guide who has called Seville home for over 30 years. My love for Spain took root long before I settled here, and every year since has deepened my understanding of this city, its culture and the way business gets done here.

Working in the travel and events industry my entire career, I founded DMC Seville Event to give corporate clients the same level of trust and local insight I built over three decades — combining Nordic reliability and precision with genuine Andalusian warmth.

As a fully licensed operator, I don't just organize events — I build programs that reflect your company's goals while giving your team an authentic, unforgettable experience of Seville and Andalusia.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions Clients Ask Before They Book

Who will be my point of contact?
Gunvor Guttormsen, founder of DMC Seville Event, and a dedicated multilingual team member for your program, from first enquiry to on-site delivery.
Which languages does your team work in?
English, Spanish and Nordic languages directly, with trusted licensed partners covering French, German, Italian and Portuguese.
Can you manage a full multi-day program, not just one activity?
Yes. From a single team building activity to a full multi-day incentive program, we plan and operate transport, venues, guides and accommodation end to end.
Where do you operate?
Seville and across Andalusia, including Málaga, Cádiz, Huelva, Granada, Córdoba, Almería and Jaén.
Are you a licensed travel and events company?
Yes. DMC Seville Event is operated by Come to Spain Events SLU, a fully licensed and registered Spanish travel agency. See our travel guarantee.
