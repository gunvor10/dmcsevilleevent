THE EXPERIENCE

Paddle Through the Past: Kayaking on the Guadalquivir in Seville

Seville is a city best explored slowly—with your senses open and your perspective fresh. While most visitors wander its shaded streets, there’s a quieter, equally enchanting way to discover its beauty: from the water.

Kayaking on the Guadalquivir River offers a completely new lens on Seville’s skyline, culture, and natural charm.

For groups, we organise everything around the paddle: certified instructors, kayaks and safety equipment, changing facilities, transfers and, if you wish, tapas or a team challenge on the riverbank afterwards.

FROM THE WATER

What you'll see and feel

Float Beneath the Bridges of Time

From your kayak, the city unfolds like a moving painting. Paddle under the Isabel II (Triana) Bridge, past the colorful facades of Calle Betis, and beneath the watchful eye of the Torre del Oro. Every stroke takes you through centuries of maritime history, connecting you with the same river that brought spices, explorers, and empires to Andalusia’s doorstep.

Nature in the Heart of the City

Though Seville is full of architectural wonders, the river is home to a quieter, more natural side of the city. Along the banks, you’ll spot birds, reeds, and flowering trees. In early morning or just before sunset, the light turns golden and the water reflects the rhythm of flamenco—not in sound, but in flow.

It’s calm, peaceful, and surprisingly rejuvenating.

A Perfect Activity for All Kinds of Groups

Whether it’s a small executive team looking for something calm, an incentive group seeking something memorable, or a large team up for a bit of fun—kayaking fits everyone.

No experience? No problem. Local instructors guide you gently from shore to shore, ensuring safety and ease.

A Photographer’s Dream

Forget crowded viewpoints. From the kayak, you’ll have uninterrupted views of Seville’s most iconic sights—from unexpected angles. Bring a waterproof camera or just enjoy the moment: the reflections, the light, and the sense of drifting right through a living postcard.

FAQ

Kayaking in Seville: common questions

Do we need kayaking experience?+

No. Local instructors give a safety briefing on land and accompany the group on the water. Kayaks are stable and suitable for beginners.

Is it safe to kayak on the Guadalquivir?+

Yes. The stretch through the city is calm, with very little current. All guests wear life jackets and are accompanied by certified instructors.

What should guests wear?+

Comfortable clothes that can get wet, sun protection and shoes that can go in the water. Changing facilities and storage are available at the base.

What is the best time of year for kayaking in Seville?+

Kayaking is possible all year. Spring and autumn are ideal; in summer we recommend early morning or sunset sessions to avoid the midday heat.

How does the raft construction activity work?+

Teams are given materials and a time limit to design and build a raft. Once finished, each team launches its raft for a race on the river. It combines planning, communication and a lot of laughter.
