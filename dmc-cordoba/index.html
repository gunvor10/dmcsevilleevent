A Meeting Point of Cultures

Córdoba was once the capital of Islamic Spain and a centre of learning for Muslims, Christians and Jews alike, leaving a historic centre layered with over a thousand years of history.

The Mezquita-Catedral

The Mezquita-Catedral, a former mosque transformed into a cathedral, is one of the most striking religious buildings in the world and the centrepiece of any Córdoba visit.

The Judería & Flower-Filled Patios

Córdoba’s Jewish Quarter, with its narrow lanes and flower-filled patios, is one of the best preserved in Europe and ideal for small-group walking activities.

A Flexible Add-On

Just over an hour from Seville, Córdoba works equally well as a half-day cultural excursion or as the base for a dedicated multi-day program.

Tapas & Patio Culture

Local tapas bars and traditional courtyard restaurants give evening programs an intimate, authentic feel distinct from Andalusia’s larger cities.

A Quieter Alternative

With fewer crowds than Seville or Granada, Córdoba suits groups looking for a more relaxed, exclusive setting without sacrificing historic weight.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Córdoba

What is the nearest airport to Córdoba?
Seville Airport (SVQ) is the nearest airport with international connections, around 140 km from Córdoba.
Is Córdoba suitable as a day trip or a full program?
Both — Córdoba works well as a half-day cultural excursion from Seville or as the base for a full multi-day incentive program.
What is the main historic site to visit in Córdoba?
The Mezquita-Catedral, a former mosque converted into a cathedral, is the city’s landmark and a highlight of any corporate itinerary.
What is the best time of year to visit Córdoba?
Spring, especially during the Patio Festival in May, and autumn offer the most comfortable temperatures — summers can be very hot.
How many people can a program in Córdoba accommodate?
Venues in and around the historic centre comfortably handle groups from 20 to 150 participants.
Can Córdoba be combined with other destinations?
Yes — Córdoba is most often combined with Seville, either as a day trip or the opening stop of a longer Andalusian program.
