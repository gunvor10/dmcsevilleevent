FAQ

Rooftop events in Seville: common questions

Can we book a rooftop exclusively for our group?+

Yes. We work with hotel and private rooftop terraces that can be reserved exclusively for your event, depending on the date and group size.

How many guests can a rooftop event host?+

Rooftop venues in Seville range from intimate terraces for around 10 guests to large spaces for up to 300. We match the venue to your group.

What happens if the weather changes?+

Seville has many sunny days, but we always plan an alternative, such as a covered area or an indoor space at the same venue.

What is the best time of year for a rooftop event?+

From March to November. In the hottest summer months we plan events for the evening, when the terraces are at their best.

Can a rooftop event be combined with other activities?+

Yes. A rooftop reception works well before a gala dinner, after a river cruise or as the closing event of an incentive programme.
