FAQ

Painting team building: common questions

Do participants need painting experience?+

No. Every session is guided by a local artist, and the activities are designed so that no one needs to be an expert. It’s about creativity and connection, not talent.

Where do the painting activities take place?+

We use patios, artist studios, rooftop terraces and ceramic workshops in Triana. We can also bring the activity to your hotel or event venue.

How large can the group be?+

Painting activities work for small teams of around 8 up to large groups of 150. The collaborative “Paint Like a Master” format works especially well for big groups.

Can guests take their artwork home?+

Yes. Individual canvases, fans and portraits can be taken home the same day. Ceramic pieces need to be fired and are delivered to your hotel or sent on afterwards. A collaborative masterpiece can be delivered to your office.

Can painting be combined with food and wine?+

Yes. Paint & Sip includes local wine and light tapas, and any other format can be followed by a tapas lunch or dinner.
