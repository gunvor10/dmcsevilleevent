TEAMWORK

Teamwork and Communication in Action

As teams navigate through Seville’s old town, they must solve clues, interpret riddles, and decide their next move together. This creates a natural environment for collaboration, strategic thinking, and open communication—far more dynamic than any indoor workshop.

The treasure hunt format encourages all participants to contribute, making it an ideal exercise for diverse teams with varying personalities and skill sets.

TEAM SPIRIT

Friendly Competition Builds Team Spirit

Who doesn’t enjoy a little rivalry? Splitting into teams adds a layer of excitement and motivation. Whether it’s racing to the next clue or cracking a puzzle faster than the others, that shared sense of purpose and energy turns colleagues into teammates—and teammates into friends.

The prize may be symbolic, but the memories created are very real.

DISCOVER SEVILLE

Discover Seville in a Playful, Immersive Way

Instead of passively listening to a guide, your group becomes the protagonist of their own adventure. Each stop along the way reveals pieces of Seville’s rich history—from Roman ruins and Moorish palaces to tucked-away plazas and hidden gardens.

It’s sightseeing, storytelling, and scavenger hunting, all rolled into one. Perfect for curious minds and energetic spirits.

CHOOSE A THEME

Themed treasure hunts

Every clue, mission and stop is built around one story of Seville. Pick a theme or let us create one for you.

Orange Theme

Follow the scent of Seville’s 40,000 orange trees, from the Patio de los Naranjos to a bitter orange marmalade or orange wine tasting at the finish.

Flamenco Theme

Clues hidden in the birthplaces of flamenco in Triana, rhythm challenges on the way and a short flamenco class as the final mission.

Painting Theme

On the trail of Murillo and Velázquez, with missions at the places they lived and painted, ending with a group painting of the city.

San Fernando III Theme

Relive the king’s entry into Seville in 1248, from the city walls to his tomb in the Royal Chapel of the Cathedral.

Aníbal González Theme

Discover the architect of the 1929 Ibero-American Exposition, with clues across Plaza de España, María Luisa Park and the pavilions.

Carmen & Opera Theme

Seville is the setting of more than 100 operas. Follow Carmen, Don Giovanni and Figaro to the places where their stories take place.

Discovery & New World Theme

Seville as the gateway to the Americas: the Torre del Oro, the Archive of the Indies and the voyage of Magellan and Elcano.

Your Company Theme

Missions, questions and the final treasure built around your company values, milestone or conference theme.

FAQ

Treasure hunts in Seville: common questions

How many people can take part?+

Treasure hunts work for groups from around 10 people up to 300. Large groups are split into teams of 5 to 8, each with its own route so the old town never gets crowded.

How long does a treasure hunt take?+

Most hunts last between 2 and 3 hours, including the briefing and the finale. The duration can be adapted to fit your programme.

Is it suitable for all fitness levels?+

Yes. The routes are on foot through the flat historic centre, and the distance can be adapted for your group.

In which languages can it be run?+

The clues, materials and facilitators can be provided in English, Spanish and other languages on request.

Can the treasure hunt have a theme?+

Yes. Popular themes include oranges, flamenco, painting, San Fernando III, Aníbal González and the operas set in Seville, such as Carmen. We can also create a theme around your company.

Can we meet historical characters on the way?+

Yes. Professional actors in costume can play characters such as Carmen, Cervantes, Velázquez or San Fernando III, each setting the teams a challenge before giving them the next clue.

What happens if it rains?+

Rain is rare in Seville, but we always have a plan B, with more indoor stops or a shorter route ending in a covered venue.
