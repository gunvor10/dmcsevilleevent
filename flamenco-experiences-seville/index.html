THE EXPERIENCE

Seville is where flamenco was born. Let your group feel it up close.

There is watching flamenco, and there is sitting two metres from the dancer as the floor shakes. We arrange the second kind: intimate, authentic performances with professional artists, staged in venues chosen for your group.

Every flamenco experience we organise is built around your programme — as the highlight of a gala dinner, a surprise welcome in a private palace, or an afternoon where your team learns the basics of compás and sevillanas from a local teacher.

We handle artists, venue, sound and lighting, catering, transport and timing, so the only thing your guests need to do is clap along.

FAQ

Flamenco for groups: common questions

Can you organise a private flamenco show just for our group?+

Yes. We book professional artists to perform exclusively for your group, in a venue we select to match your group size and programme.

What is the minimum and maximum group size?+

We organise flamenco experiences for groups from around 10 guests up to several hundred. The format and venue are adapted to the size of your group.

Do guests need dance experience for the workshop?+

No. The workshop is designed for complete beginners and focuses on rhythm, clapping and simple steps, so everyone can take part.

Can flamenco be combined with a dinner or other activities?+

Yes. Flamenco works well as part of a gala dinner, a welcome reception or an evening tapas route, and we coordinate the full programme.

How far in advance should we book?+

For large groups and popular dates, we recommend contacting us at least two to three months ahead. Smaller groups can often be arranged at shorter notice.
