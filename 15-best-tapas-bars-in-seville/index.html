15 Best Tapas Bars in Seville

Where can we eat like a local? A personal guide to Seville's tapas bars, from the oldest in the city to the new favourites.

One of the questions I get asked the most is: Where can we eat like a local? The answer: tapas bars. Not fancy restaurants, not tourist traps—just cozy, often loud, gloriously chaotic tapas joints where life spills out onto the street.

Make tapas hopping an important part of your stay in Seville. You will not regret it.

Tapas are all about picar, nibbling, sharing, and moving on. Like a culinary flamenco—quick, vibrant, and full of flair. Here are some of my all-time favorites, both the most antique and some of the new ones.

DID YOU KNOW?

Sevilla has the most tapas bars per inhabitant in all of Spain.

MY FAVOURITES

The 15 bars

01
El Rinconcillo CLASSIC

The oldest bar in Seville (since 1670!).

Try: espinacas con garbanzos (spinach with chickpeas) and a cold beer
02
Eslava MODERN

A little more modern, but their honey-glazed ribs are legendary. Worth the wait.

Try: the honey-glazed ribs
03
Bodega Santa Cruz (Las Columnas) CLASSIC

Right by the Cathedral, and somehow still authentic.

Try: solomillo al whisky
04
La Azotea MODERN

Great for fresh seafood.

Try: the tuna tartare or their seasonal specials
05
Casa Morales CLASSIC

Old-school charm, cured meats hanging overhead, and great montaditos (mini sandwiches).

Try: montaditos
06
Ovejas Negras MODERN

Trendy and creative.

Try: truffled eggplant and mini burgers
07
Dos de Mayo CLASSIC

Beloved by locals for big portions and great prices.

Try: the grilled octopus
08
La Brunilda MODERN

Upscale tapas in a relaxed setting.

Try: prawn tempura or Iberian pork cheek
09
Contenedor MODERN

A slow food kitchen with a rotating menu of bold flavors.

Try: whatever is on the menu today
10
Blanca Paloma TRIANA

Traditional Triana favorite with strong local flavor.

Try: fried fish and salmorejo
11
Bar Alfalfa CLASSIC

Small, lively, and full of charm.

Try: a glass of wine and some jamón
12
Las Golondrinas TRIANA

Hidden in Triana, their presa Ibérica is famous.

Try: presa Ibérica
13
Vinería San Telmo WINE

Extensive wine list and top-tier tapas with a creative touch.

Try: a wine pairing with the chef’s tapas
14
Taberna Peregil WINE

A classic for Seville’s famous orange wine.

Try: vino de naranja with a tapa of chorizo or queso viejo
15
La Bartola MODERN

Modern Andalusian fusion and a stylish atmosphere.

Try: the Andalusian fusion dishes
DO YOU KNOW HOW TO ORDER TAPAS?

How to order tapas like a local

01

Stand at the bar if you can.

02

Order one or two things at a time and share.

03

Don’t be afraid to ask for recommendations.
