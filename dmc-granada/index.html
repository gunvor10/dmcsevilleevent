ABOUT GRANADA

Granada — Where the Alhambra Meets the Sierra Nevada

World Heritage Alhambra palace complex
Mountain backdrop for adventure activities
Rich Moorish and Andalusian heritage
Atmospheric Albaicín and Sacromonte quarters
A dramatic setting for gala dinners
A UNESCO World Heritage City

Granada’s historic centre and the Alhambra palace complex are inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, giving any corporate program a backdrop of exceptional cultural weight.

The Alhambra & Generalife

The Alhambra, a fortress-palace built by the Nasrid dynasty, and its Generalife gardens draw visitors from across the world; private guided access can be arranged for groups of any size.

The Albaicín & Sacromonte

The Albaicín’s steep, whitewashed streets and the cave-dwelling Sacromonte quarter, birthplace of Zambra flamenco, offer atmospheric settings for walking activities and evening flamenco shows.

Mountains on the Doorstep

The Sierra Nevada range rises directly behind the city, giving incentive groups quick access to hiking, skiing and mountain excursions rarely available so close to a historic centre.

Tapas Culture

Granada is known for serving free tapas with every drink, making informal group dinners in the historic centre an easy, authentic addition to any program.

Well Connected

Federico García Lorca Granada-Jaén Airport, and Granada’s proximity to Málaga’s international airport, give planners flexible arrival options for groups.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Granada

What is the best airport for reaching Granada?
Málaga Airport (AGP) offers the best international connections, with a transfer of approximately 1 hour 30 minutes by bus to Granada.
Is it possible to visit the Alhambra with a large corporate group?
Yes, with advance booking we secure private guided access to the Alhambra and Generalife gardens for groups of any size.
What team building options does Granada offer?
Options include Albaicín treasure hunts, flamenco workshops and Sierra Nevada mountain excursions.
What is the best time of year for an incentive trip to Granada?
Spring and autumn offer mild weather for exploring the Alhambra and Albaicín, while winter adds the option of skiing in the Sierra Nevada.
How many people can DMC Seville Event handle for a program in Granada?
We organise groups from small executive retreats to over 200 participants, adapting venues and Alhambra access accordingly.
Can Granada be combined with other Andalusian destinations?
Yes — Granada pairs naturally with Almería or the Costa del Sol as part of a multi-destination itinerary.
