Group Transfers & Ground Transportation for MICE in Seville

DMC Seville Event is a local destination management company that organises group transfers and ground transportation for event agencies, corporate groups, congresses and incentive trips in Seville, with excursion transfers across Andalusia. We offer airport and train station arrivals, coaches and minibuses with professional drivers, VIP vans, shuttles between hotels, venues and dinners, and transfers for excursions in Andalusia. For special transfers we also arrange river boats, horse-drawn carriages and classic cars. Every transfer is planned in advance, matched to your group size and programme, and followed up on the day by our team.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Group & Event Transfers in Seville

Can you handle only the transfers for our event in Seville?
Yes. Many event agencies organise the programme themselves and ask us to manage the transport locally.
How large a group can you handle?
We plan transfers for small groups and for large events with several coaches, matching vehicles and timings to your programme.
Do you organise airport transfers for event groups from Seville Airport?
Yes, we organise meet & greet and group transfers from Seville Airport (SVQ) to hotels and venues in Seville and the rest of Andalusia.
Can you organise transfers from Málaga or Jerez airport?
Yes, we can arrange group transfers from Málaga (AGP) and Jerez (XRY) airports to Seville.
What happens if flights are delayed?
We follow flight times and adjust pick-ups, and our coordinator keeps you updated on the day.
How far in advance should we book event transfers?
We recommend booking as early as possible, especially in spring, during Semana Santa and the Feria de Abril.
