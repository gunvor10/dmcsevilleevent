FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Tailor Made Trips Seville

What makes a tailor made trip to Seville different from a package tour?
We don't offer pre-made packages — every itinerary is built around your interests, pace and preferences, from private guides to hidden neighborhoods most visitors never see.
Who are tailor made trips in Seville designed for?
Private travelers, couples and small groups looking for a personalized, one-of-a-kind travel experience in Seville and greater Andalusia.
What activities can be included in a tailor made trip?
Private cultural tours, tapas tastings, flamenco shows and workshops, day trips to Córdoba, Ronda or Jerez, luxury accommodation, and exclusive access to monuments or hidden local spots.
What languages does DMC Seville Event support?
Our team provides support in English, Spanish, French and Italian.
How far in advance should we plan a tailor made trip?
We recommend starting the conversation at least 4-6 weeks ahead for a fully custom itinerary, though shorter timelines can often be accommodated.
Can a tailor made trip include day trips outside Seville?
Yes — Córdoba, Ronda, Jerez and the White Villages are all popular additions, fully coordinated as part of your itinerary.
