Cocktail and Event Receptions for MICE Groups in Seville

A cocktail reception is a perfect way to open or close an event in Seville. Guests meet, talk and enjoy local food and drinks in a setting that shows the best of the city. DMC Seville Event is a local destination management company that organises cocktail receptions for event agencies, DMCs, congresses and incentive groups of every size, on rooftops with views of the Giralda, in palace courtyards, in gardens and on boats on the Guadalquivir. We take care of the venue, catering, drinks, music and staff, and you have one contact person from planning to the last guest.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Cocktail & Event Receptions in Seville

Where can we hold a cocktail reception in Seville?
On rooftops, in palaces and courtyards, in gardens, at haciendas and on boats on the Guadalquivir.
How many guests can a cocktail reception accommodate?
We find venues for small groups and large receptions. Capacity depends on the venue we choose for you.
What food is served at a cocktail reception?
Canapés, tapas and food stations with Andalusian and international dishes, confirmed with you in advance.
Can you accommodate dietary requirements?
Yes, our catering partners accommodate dietary restrictions and preferences, confirmed with you in advance.
Can a cocktail reception be combined with a gala dinner?
Yes, many groups start with drinks on a rooftop or in a courtyard and continue to dinner.
How far in advance should we book a cocktail reception?
We recommend booking 2-3 months ahead, especially for rooftops in spring and autumn.
