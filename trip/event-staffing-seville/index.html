Frequently Asked Questions — Event Staffing in Seville

What types of event staff do you provide in Seville?
Almost any event staff you need: hostesses, registration staff, guides, coordinators, interpreters, tour leaders, waiters and promotional staff.
What languages do your event staff speak?
We provide staff in the most common languages. For more specific languages, send us a request.
Do you provide freelance event staff in Seville?
Yes. We work with experienced freelance event staff in Seville. We select, brief and manage them for you, so you deal with one company and one contact person.
Can we book event staff without other services?
Yes, staff can be booked on their own or together with venues, transfers and activities.
Do event staff wear uniforms?
We agree the dress code with you. Staff can wear their own uniform, your branded clothing or a dress code you choose.
How many staff do we need?
It depends on group size and programme. We recommend the right number when we prepare your proposal.
How far in advance should we book event staff?
We recommend booking at least a few weeks ahead, and earlier for large congresses and busy periods.
